Understanding cannabis purchase limits

How much cannabis you can buy in one transaction in New Jersey, by product type, from the official sources.

Adult-use limits per transaction

In each transaction, a dispensary may sell you up to the equivalent of 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of usable cannabis. In one transaction you can buy up to 28.35 grams (1 ounce) of dried flower. In one transaction you can buy up to 4 grams of solid cannabis concentrate or resin, or the equivalent of 4 grams of concentrate in liquid form. In one transaction you can buy up to 1,000 mg of ingestible cannabis products such as gummies, for example ten 100 mg packages. Source: Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens in a new tab)

Two official texts give different figures here. Both are shown, each with its source.

In one transaction you can buy up to 4 grams of vaporized formulations (oil). Source: Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens in a new tab)

The rules also cap a single sale at four grams of solid concentrate or four mL of liquid concentrate (oil), and at vaporized formulations containing no more than four mL of oil. Source: N.J.A.C. 17:30 Personal Use Cannabis Rules (Supp. 3-6-23) (opens in a new tab)

Under the state rules, a retailer may not sell more than 28.35 grams (one ounce) of usable cannabis to a consumer in a single transaction. The rules cap a single sale of ingestible products at an aggregate total of 1,000 mg of THC. Source: N.J.A.C. 17:30 Personal Use Cannabis Rules (Supp. 3-6-23) (opens in a new tab)

Combining products

You can combine products in one purchase, for example 14.17 grams (1/2 ounce) of dried flower and 2 grams of concentrate, or 5 packages of gummies and 14.17 grams (1/2 ounce) of dried flower. Source: Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens in a new tab)

A combination purchase may not exceed 28.35 grams (one ounce) or the equivalent, and retailers must make a good-faith effort to stop customers from going over one ounce across multiple transactions. Source: N.J.A.C. 17:30 Personal Use Cannabis Rules (Supp. 3-6-23) (opens in a new tab)

How much you can possess

Two official texts give different figures here. Both are shown, each with its source.

New Jersey state law allows adults 21 and older to have up to 6 ounces of cannabis and cannabis products in their possession. Source: Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens in a new tab); General Information: Legal Cannabis in New Jersey (opens in a new tab)

The state rules say it is not unlawful for a consumer to possess, purchase, transport or give away without payment up to 1 ounce of usable cannabis, or the equivalent in cannabis products, obtained directly from a licensed retailer. Source: N.J.A.C. 17:30 Personal Use Cannabis Rules (Supp. 3-6-23) (opens in a new tab)

You may give up to 1 ounce (28.35 grams) of cannabis to another adult 21 or older, but you may not accept any payment or compensation for it. Source: Recreational Cannabis in New Jersey (opens in a new tab)

Medical patients

Two official texts give different figures here. Both are shown, each with its source.

The most a patient or designated caregiver may buy is 85 grams (3 oz) for a 30-day period, the practitioner sets the dosage, and terminally ill patients are exempt from the maximum. Source: Patient FAQs (opens in a new tab)

The same patient FAQ gives the maximum monthly allowance, taken as flower, as 84 grams, or 3 ounces. Source: Patient FAQs (opens in a new tab)

Patients may buy up to 85 grams (3 oz) every 30 days, while recreational consumers may buy only 28.35 grams (1 oz) in a single transaction. Patients may buy any combination of flower, concentrates, oil and ingestibles as long as they stay within their recommendation's monthly limit. Source: Patient FAQs (opens in a new tab)
