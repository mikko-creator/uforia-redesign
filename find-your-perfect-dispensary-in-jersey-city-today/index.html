3. Complete Product Guide: Everything Available at Uforia

Uforia carries nine product categories covering every consumption method, experience level, and wellness goal. All products are sourced from NJ-licensed producers, third-party tested, and stocked with full CoA documentation. Below is a complete breakdown of what you’ll find on the menu.

Cannabis Flower

What to Know Before You Buy

Flower is the most direct and controllable form of cannabis. Because it is inhaled, onset occurs within 2 to 10 minutes and effects typically last 1 to 3 hours making it the easiest category to self-titrate, particularly for new consumers.

Terpene Science: Terpenes are aromatic compounds that shape cannabis effects independently of THC content a phenomenon called the entourage effect. Limonene = mood elevation; myrcene = sedation; pinene = mental clarity; caryophyllene = stress relief. Always ask Uforia staff for terpene-forward recommendations.

Cannabis Edibles

What to Know Before You Buy

Edibles are metabolised through the liver, where THC converts to 11-hydroxy-THC a compound with stronger and longer-lasting effects than inhaled cannabis. Onset ranges from 45 to 90 minutes depending on metabolism, body weight, and food intake. Duration is 4 to 8 hours, significantly longer than flower or vapes.

Formats available:

  • Gummies (micro-dose and standard; fruit and botanical flavours)
  • Chocolates (dark, milk, white)
  • Infused beverages (sparkling, tonic, functional wellness)
  • Capsules (flavourless; precise dosing)

Dosing Guide:

Cannabis Concentrates

THC concentration: 60 to 90%. Best for experienced consumers. Onset within 1 to 3 minutes via dabbing or vaporisation.

Cannabis Vaporizers

Onset: 2 to 10 minutes. No combustion. Discreet and portable.

Buying criteria:

  • Choose strain-specific live resin cartridges for the most accurate terpene and effect profile
  • Verify the CoA on every cartridge unverified vapes are the most common source of contaminant exposure in the cannabis market
  • Both 510-thread cartridges and all-in-one disposable pens are available at Uforia

Cannabis Tinctures

Sublingual (under-tongue) absorption. Onset: 15 to 45 minutes. Duration: 2 to 4 hours.

Best for: Precise, repeatable dosing; medical-adjacent consumers; mixing with food or drinks; daytime microdosing.

CBD and Wellness Products

High-CBD, low-THC products for therapeutic benefit without psychoactive effect.

Cannabis Topicals

Applied directly to skin. No psychoactive effect even with high-THC formulations. Cannabis compounds do not enter the bloodstream through topical application.

Best for: Joint pain, muscle soreness, post-workout recovery, and localised skin conditions.

Key Takeaway: Uforia’s product range covers every consumption method and experience level from 2.5mg microdose gummies to solventless rosin for experienced concentrate users.

6. Responsible Use and Safety Guidelines

Universal Rules Every Consumer Should Follow

First-Timer Checklist

Before your first visit or first session:

  • Choose a high-CBD, low-THC strain (under 15% THC for flower)
  • Start with flower or tincture not edibles
  • If trying edibles: begin at 5mg THC maximum
  • Wait the full 90-minute onset window before reassessing
  • Tell your Uforia budtender it’s your first time they’ll adjust every recommendation
  • Have water, food, and a familiar space ready before you consume
  • Avoid dabbing or concentrates until you have established your tolerance

Experienced Consumer Recommendations

  • Explore terpene-forward craft flower effects go far beyond raw THC percentage
  • Try solventless concentrates (rosin, bubble hash) for the cleanest extraction experience
  • Use microdosing (2.5mg THC per session) for functional all-day benefit without impairment
  • Ask about entourage effect formulations specific cannabinoid and terpene combinations targeting sleep, focus, or pain relief

Key Takeaway: The most common adverse cannabis experience whether for beginners or veterans is preventable with correct dosing and informed product selection.

10. Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a medical card to shop at Uforia?

No. Uforia serves both recreational and medical customers. Any adult 21 or older with valid government-issued photo ID can purchase cannabis without a medical card under New Jersey’s Adult Use Cannabis Law, which took effect in April 2022.

What payment methods does Uforia accept?

Cash and PIN-based debit cards are accepted in-store. Online orders support ACH payment via AeroPay and debit cards. Credit cards are not accepted due to federal banking restrictions this is an industry-wide limitation affecting all NJ dispensaries, not a store policy. An on-site ATM is available for cash withdrawals.

How do I place an order for in-store pickup?

Visit uforiadispensary.com, browse the live menu, add items to your cart, select a pickup window, and complete checkout. Bring your valid 21+ government photo ID and order confirmation to 138 Griffith St. Express pickup is typically completed in under 5 minutes.

What should I bring when visiting Uforia?

A valid, unexpired government-issued photo ID confirming you are 21 or older. If you placed an online order, bring your confirmation number. No prior registration or medical card is required.

How do I verify my product is genuinely lab-tested?

Locate the NJ Universal Symbol on the packaging. Scan the QR code or enter the URL on the label to access the product’s Certificate of Analysis, which displays verified cannabinoid percentages and confirms clearance of pesticides, heavy metals, and microbial contaminants.

What are Uforia’s delivery eligibility requirements?

Delivery is available to NJ residential addresses only. Orders must be pre-paid online. The recipient must be 21+ with valid ID verified at the door. Deliveries cannot be made to vehicles, public spaces, parks, or commercial offices. Delivery hours: Mon to Thu 11AM to 9PM, Fri to Sat 10AM to 10PM, Sun 10AM to 8PM.

What is the difference between live resin and standard concentrate?

Live resin is extracted from fresh-frozen plant material harvested immediately after cutting preserving the full terpene spectrum that would otherwise degrade during drying and curing. Standard concentrates are produced from dried material, resulting in measurable terpene loss. Live resin consistently delivers richer flavour and a more complete entourage effect.

How should I store cannabis products at home?

Store in a cool, dark, dry location away from direct sunlight, heat, and humidity. Airtight glass containers preserve flower freshness and terpene integrity best. Keep all products in original child-resistant packaging, clearly labelled, and out of reach of children and pets.

Can someone else accept my delivery?

Yes, provided the recipient is 21+ and presents a valid government-issued photo ID at handoff. The delivery driver will verify ID as required under NJ-CRC delivery regulations before completing the transaction.

Why is cannabis delivery discreet?

Uforia uses unmarked, NJ-CRC approved delivery vehicles and employs licensed delivery personnel trained in all state compliance protocols. Your order is handed directly to you at the door following ID verification never left unattended.
