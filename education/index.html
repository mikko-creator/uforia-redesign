KNOWLEDGE IS POWER

As a recreational cannabis consumer in New Jersey, staying informed about the latest developments in the industry and changes to state laws is crucial. Follow reputable sources, such as the New Jersey government website and trusted news outlets, to ensure you’re always up to date. By educating yourself about the laws, safe consumption practices, and how to choose the right products, you can enjoy the benefits of recreational cannabis in New Jersey responsibly and safely.

NEW JERSEY CANNABIS REGULATORY COMMISSION

The New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory Commission seeks to establish and grow a responsible, regulated medicinal and recreational cannabis industry. By applying the values of safety and equity, the Commission will center its work around creating and protecting access for patients, the production of safe products, and promoting diversity and inclusion in the industry to develop a national model for sensible, fair oversight.

NEW JERSEY ADDICTION HOTLINE

ReachNJ is a central call-in line for New Jersey residents who are looking for help with a substance use disorder (SUD). Each call to ReachNJ is answered by a live person in the first 30 seconds. Trained staff will screen callers to identify their exact needs, are able to provide referrals to supportive services and a seamless connection to a local treatment provider. ReachNJ serves NJ residents of all ages regardless of insurance status or ability to pay.

National Organization for the Reform of Marijuana Laws

NORML’s mission is to move public opinion sufficiently to legalize the responsible use of marijuana by adults, and to serve as an advocate for consumers to assure they have access to high quality marijuana that is safe, convenient and affordable. Since its founding in 1970, NORML has provided a voice in the public policy debate for those Americans who oppose marijuana prohibition and favor an end to the practice of arresting marijuana consumers. A nonprofit public-interest advocacy group, NORML represents the interests of the tens of millions of Americans who use marijuana responsibly.

HUDSON COUNTY COMMUNITY COLLEGE

Hudson County Community College (HCCC) in New Jersey has recognized the growing significance and opportunities within the cannabis industry by introducing an innovative Cannabis Education program. This program is designed to equip students with the necessary knowledge and skills to thrive in various sectors of the cannabis market. The curriculum includes comprehensive coverage of cannabis law, cultivation, dispensary operations, and the business aspects of the cannabis industry. By providing a blend of theoretical knowledge and practical skills, HCCC aims to prepare graduates for successful careers in this rapidly expanding field, addressing the need for educated professionals as the legal landscape around cannabis continues to evolve. This educational initiative highlights the college’s commitment to staying at the forefront of emerging industries and workforce development.

Essential New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory & Advocacy Resources

Heady NJ is an independent news site that covers cannabis news at the local level in New Jersey. The site focuses on the ongoing battle for the legalization of medical and adult-use cannabis, hemp, and psychedelics, as well as the emerging businesses and culture associated with these movements. Heady NJ reports on the latest New Jersey cannabis news, political developments, and updates at dispensaries. It also provides a platform for its members to review the latest products and services at each NJ cannabis dispensary, including those in the nearby TriState area. The team at Heady NJ brings together a wealth of knowledge from the cannabis community. Their partners have been pioneers in the cannabis market since the early 90s. Heady NJ aims to deliver the best in New Jersey cannabis news, information, culture, events, and reviews, establishing itself as the ultimate hub for all things cannabis in New Jersey.

MARIJUANA BUSINESS DAILY

MJ Biz Daily is a leading business news resource for the cannabis industry, providing timely and in-depth coverage tailored to industry professionals. The website offers a wide array of content, including breaking news, analysis, and comprehensive reports on regulatory developments, market trends, and business strategies. It also features insights into different segments of the cannabis market from cultivation and production to retail and ancillary services. MJ Biz Daily is particularly renowned for its annual MJBizCon, the largest gathering of cannabis business professionals in the world. This platform serves as a critical resource for anyone looking to stay informed and competitive in the rapidly evolving cannabis industry.

View More

NEW JERSEY CANNA BUSINESS ASSOCIATION

The New Jersey CannaBusiness Association is a non-profit trade organization committed to fostering a responsible, sustainable, and inclusive cannabis industry. Initially focusing on advocating for medical cannabis and patients’ rights, the NJCBA spearheaded initiatives to end cannabis prohibition, create procedures for expungements, and dismantle longstanding stigmas. Presently, the NJCBA remains actively engaged with state lawmakers and regulatory bodies to promote the development of a strong and conscientious industry that generates employment and benefits the communities in which it operates.
