Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions below.

How can I confirm that the cannabis product I purchased is regulated?

To verify the cannabis product you purchased is a regulated, lab tested, high-quality product from a licensed dispensary, look for the universal symbol (pictured to the right) on the packaging of your cannabis product(s). Consumers can also scan the QR code or visit the URL on the packaging of a product to verify the certificate of analysis (CoA) that proves the product was lab tested.

Is there an age requirement to purchase cannabis products?

Yes. You must be 21 years or older to purchase adult-use cannabis products. Adult-use cannabis products cannot be sold or delivered to individuals under twenty-one years of age.

How do I place an Order for delivery?

You can place a delivery order 24/7 at www.uforiadispensary.com or by calling us at

What times are available for delivery?

Our cannabis deliveries are made to your doorstep daily from 12-8pm

Where can I receive a delivery?

Deliveries must be made to:

  • A physical home address within the State of New Jersey; and to
  • A customer (21+), who is located at all forms of residential properties; short and long- term residencies, anywhere in NJ.
  • Deliveries are not permitted anywhere else, including but not limited to public building, public spaces including parks; community centers, any school grounds, day-care centers; and houses of worship, offices or to a motor vehicle.
Can my order be delivered to my car?

No. Deliveries may not be made to anyone inside of a motor vehicle.

Is identification (ID) verification required to place a delivery order?

Yes. When placing an order online for delivery, customers must attest that they are of legal age (21+) to consume cannabis at time of purchase. At time of delivery, an additional identification check must be carried out by the employee making the delivery to complete the order.

What is considered valid identification (ID)?

  • A valid federal, state, or local government identification, stating the customer’s age and a photograph of the individual’s face.

  • A valid driver’s license or non-driver identification card issued by the department of motor vehicles, the federal government, any United States territory, commonwealth or possession, the District of Columbia, a state government within the United States or a provincial government of the dominion of Canada
  • A valid passport issued by the United States government or any other country
  • An identification card issued by the armed forces of the United States.
Is cash accepted upon delivery?

No. All orders must be pre-paid on our website prior to delivery.

How do I pay for my delivery order?

All orders must be pre-paid prior to delivery by using one of our easy payment options:

  • ACH payment: select the AeroPay option at checkout and you will receive a secure payment link via text from our payment processor AeroPay. Please note payment links will only be sent during the hours we are open daily (12p-8p).
  • Debit cards: Select the credit / debit option at checkout.
How will my order be delivered?

Unmarked delivery vehicle approved for Cannabis deliveries in NJ.

How do I track the status of my delivery order?

Please keep your phone turned on with the sound on. We’ll contact you via text message with updates about your order. You can also track the status by going to the activity page of your profile.

Can I designate someone else to accept my delivery?

Yes. But if the individual placing the order will not be the individual accepting the order, the individual accepting the order must be 21+ year old and must show valid identification, as it is described above, when they accept the delivery.

Will I receive an invoice or receipt with my order?

Yes. Customers will receive a receipt at the time of delivery. This will include our dispensary information (name and license number), the name and quantity of products delivered, and the name of the delivery employee, and other relevant information.

Is a signature required upon delivery?

Yes. ID verification and a signature from the customer for each order is required.

Is there a delivery fee?

Delivery fees may apply. Our business proudly offers low delivery fees to make our products and services more accessible and affordable for everyone. By keeping our delivery costs minimal, we ensure that you can enjoy the convenience of home delivery without the burden of high fees. This approach helps us maintain competitive pricing and enhances overall customer satisfaction, making it easier for you to choose our services whenever you need them.
