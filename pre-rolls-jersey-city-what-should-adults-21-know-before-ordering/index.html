What Are Pre Rolls and Why Do Adults Choose Them?

Pre rolls are cannabis flower that has already been ground and rolled into paper or a cone. Adults choose them for convenience, consistent size and easy sharing. They also let a new customer try a strain without buying a full eighth.

Retail data from Hoodie Analytics shows New Jersey pre roll sales grew 22.8% year over year as of April 2026. Pre rolls now hold nearly 18% of the state market, so nearly every dispensary menu treats them as a core category.

What Types of Pre Rolls Jersey City Dispensaries Stock?

Menus usually sort pre rolls by size and format:

  • Singles: 0.5 g, 0.7 g and 1 g joints for one session
  • Multi-packs: 2-pack to 10-pack options for sharing or stocking up
  • Infused pre rolls: flower coated or blended with oil or concentrate
  • Kief-coated pre rolls: flower dusted with kief for added strength
  • Strain types: indica, sativa and hybrid

Each listing shows weight, strain type and THC percentage. Stock changes by brand and batch, so the live menu is the most accurate source.

What Are Diamond Infused Pre Rolls?

Diamond infused pre rolls combine cannabis flower with THCa diamonds, which are crystalline THCa, often together with an oil or kief layer. The added concentrate raises potency and usually slows the burn.

Strength varies by product, so read the listed THC percentage and weight first. Start with a small amount and wait before using more. Diamond infused pre rolls often cost more than standard pre rolls, so compare price per gram.

How Do You Choose the Best Prerolls for Your Needs?

No single product suits every adult, so choose the best prerolls by comparing facts:

  1. Weight and pack size: a 0.5 g single suits a first try, while a 5-pack suits regular use.
  2. Strain type: indica, sativa and hybrid labels are a starting point, but effects differ by person.
  3. THC percentage: a higher number means a stronger product, not a better one.
  4. Format: standard, infused or diamond infused.
  5. Lab testing and labels: licensed New Jersey products are lab tested, and the label shows batch details.
  6. Price per gram: divide the price by the weight to compare packs.

What Are the New Jersey Rules for Buying Pre Rolls?

Pre rolls Jersey City adults buy follow state law:

  • Age: you must be 21 or older with a valid government photo ID.
  • Residency: New Jersey residency is not required.
  • Purchase limit: up to 1 ounce of flower or its equivalent per transaction, and pre roll weight counts toward that limit.
  • Possession limit: adults may possess up to 6 ounces.
  • Use: never drive impaired, and keep cannabis away from children and pets.

Rules can change, so check the New Jersey Cannabis Regulatory Commission for current limits.
